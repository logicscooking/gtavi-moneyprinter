# gtavi-moneyprinter playbook

## Daily block (60-90 min)
1. `campaigns rank`: pick today's campaign (highest $/hour with budget left).
2. `trends` + `streams`: pick 2 sources the campaign allows.
3. `run "<url>" --top 3 --campaign <page>`: 3-6 clips. Discard any FAIL.
4. Rewrite hooks if a sharper line exists (`hook-lab`). Post hook 1 + cover 1.
5. `calendar` for the rest of the day; post by hand at each slot.
6. `learn` every post with its URL. Next day, add views; after 72 h, add earnings.
7. One question from the comments -> one HOW TO post (`questions-to-posts`).

## Launch week (from Nov 19)
- Day 0-1: every 2 hours, 08:00-22:00. NZ/AU streamers unlock first: clip them, X first, then TikTok/Reels/Shorts.
- Day 2-6: 4 posts/day. Mix: 60% campaign clips, 20% tips/secrets, 20% translated clips.
- No story spoilers in hooks or covers during launch week.

## Content mix after launch
- Campaign clips (paid per 1K views).
- Secrets map tips (verified only).
- Cheat carousel (reported codes, sourced).
- Translated clips (ES/PT/DE/RU/FR/JA pages).

## Clip rules
- Hook in the first second; under 8 words.
- 7-35 seconds; start just before the action, end on the payoff.
- Big captions in the safe area; most people watch muted.
- At least 2 transformations + "via @creator". Never a plain re-upload.
- One test per day. Judge after 24 h, trust 72 h.

## Money, honestly
Campaigns pay per 1,000 approved views at the rate each page shows. Platforms don't pay for reposted clips. No income is promised; the dashboard shows only what you actually earned.
